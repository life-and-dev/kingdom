# Booths / Tabernacles

One-event draft. Shared calendar and cross-feast evidence remain in [Feasts](../feasts.draft.md).

## Biblical names and status

Feast of Booths (Leviticus 23:34); Feast of Tabernacles (Deuteronomy 16:16; John 7:2).

## Purpose and symbolism

Leviticus 23:42–43: Israel dwelt in booths after Egypt.

## When

Leviticus 23:34: fifteenth day of seventh month for seven days.

## Requirements

### Food

No feast-specific command stated.

### Sacrifices/offerings

Leviticus 23:35–36; Numbers 29:12–39, including “in addition to your vow offerings and your freewill offerings”.

### Assembly/place/private-public

“Three times a year all your males shall appear before the LORD your God at the place that he will choose: at the Feast of Unleavened Bread, at the Feast of Weeks, and at the Feast of Booths. They shall not appear before the LORD empty-handed.” — [Deuteronomy 16:16 (ESV)](https://www.esv.org/Deuteronomy+16%3A16/)

Leviticus 23:40,42 names fruit of splendid trees, rejoicing, and booths.

### Fasting/self-affliction

No feast-specific command stated.

### Rest/work

Leviticus 23:35.

## Forever clause and audience

Leviticus 23:41; booth command names native Israelites.

## Biblical celebration examples

1 Kings 8:2,65–66; 2 Chronicles 5:3; 7:8–10; 8:13; Ezra 3:4; Nehemiah 8:14–18; Zechariah 14:16–19; John 7:2,8–14,37–39; 8:12.
