# Weekly Sabbath

Comparable weekly observance draft. Shared cross-feast evidence remains in [Feasts](../feasts.draft.md).

## Biblical names and status

Weekly Sabbath, not Torah annual appointed feast.

## Purpose and symbolism

Genesis 2:1–3; Exodus 20:8–11.

## When

Every seventh day.

## Requirements

### Food

No feast-specific command stated.

### Sacrifices/offerings

Numbers 28:9–10.

### Assembly/place/private-public

Leviticus 23:3.

### Fasting/self-affliction

No feast-specific command stated.

### Rest/work

Exodus 20:8–11; 31:12–17; 35:1–3.

## Forever clause and audience

Exodus 31:12–17: covenant forever with people of Israel.

## Biblical celebration examples

Isaiah 56:2–7; 58:13–14; Ezekiel 20:12–24; Mark 2:23–28; Luke 4:16; Acts 13:14–44; 16:13; 17:2; 18:4.

## Every feast-specific scripture

> “Thus the heavens and the earth were finished, and all the host of them. And on the seventh day God finished his work that he had done, and he rested on the seventh day from all his work that he had done. So God blessed the seventh day and made it holy, because on it God rested from all his work that he had done in creation.” — [Genesis 2:1–3 (ESV)](https://www.esv.org/Genesis+2%3A1-3/)

> “The LORD spoke to Moses, saying, ‘Speak to the people of Israel and say to them, These are the appointed feasts of the LORD that you shall proclaim as holy convocations; they are my appointed feasts. Six days shall work be done, but on the seventh day is a Sabbath of solemn rest, a holy convocation. You shall do no work. It is a Sabbath to the LORD in all your dwelling places.’” — [Leviticus 23:1–3 (ESV)](https://www.esv.org/Leviticus+23%3A1-3/)

> “And he said to them, ‘The Sabbath was made for man, not man for the Sabbath. So the Son of Man is lord even of the Sabbath.’” — [Mark 2:27–28 (ESV)](https://www.esv.org/Mark+2%3A27-28/)

> “Therefore let no one pass judgment on you in questions of food and drink, or with regard to a festival or a new moon or a Sabbath. These are a shadow of the things to come, but the substance belongs to Christ.” — [Colossians 2:16–17 (ESV)](https://www.esv.org/Colossians+2%3A16-17/)

> “On the sixth day they gathered twice as much bread, two omers each. … ‘Tomorrow is a day of solemn rest, a holy Sabbath to the LORD; … Six days you shall gather it, but on the seventh day, which is a Sabbath, there will be none.’ … ‘Let no one go out of his place on the seventh day.’ So the people rested on the seventh day.” — [Exodus 16:22–30 (ESV)](https://www.esv.org/Exodus+16%3A22-30/)

> “Remember the Sabbath day, to keep it holy. Six days you shall labor, and do all your work, but the seventh day is a Sabbath to the LORD your God. … For in six days the LORD made the heavens and the earth, the sea, and all that is in them, and rested on the seventh day. Therefore the LORD blessed the Sabbath day and made it holy.” — [Exodus 20:8–11 (ESV)](https://www.esv.org/Exodus+20%3A8-11/)

> “And the LORD said to Moses, ‘You are to speak to the people of Israel and say, “Above all you shall keep my Sabbaths, for this is a sign between me and you throughout your generations. … Everyone who profanes it shall be put to death. Whoever does any work on it, that soul shall be cut off from among his people. … The people of Israel shall keep the Sabbath … throughout their generations, as a covenant forever. It is a sign forever between me and the people of Israel.”’” — [Exodus 31:12–17 (ESV)](https://www.esv.org/Exodus+31%3A12-17/)

> “So then, there remains a Sabbath rest for the people of God, for whoever has entered God’s rest has also rested from his works as God did from his. Let us therefore strive to enter that rest, so that no one may fall by the same sort of disobedience.” — [Hebrews 4:9–11 (ESV)](https://www.esv.org/Hebrews+4%3A9-11/)

Prophetic and narrative references may describe Sabbath keeping or use Sabbath language; they do not supply a comprehensive modern-work definition. Temple offerings remain stated as offerings, not silently replaced by private rites.

### Exodus 35:1–3

> “Moses assembled all the congregation of the people of Israel and said to them, ‘These are the things that the LORD has commanded you to do. Six days work shall be done, but on the seventh day you shall have a Sabbath of solemn rest, holy to the LORD. … You shall kindle no fire in all your dwelling places on the Sabbath day.’” — [Exodus 35:1–3 (ESV)](https://www.esv.org/Exodus+35%3A1-3/)

### Deuteronomy 5:12–15

> “Observe the Sabbath day, to keep it holy, as the LORD your God commanded you. … You shall not do any work … You shall remember that you were a slave in the land of Egypt, and the LORD your God brought you out from there with a mighty hand and an outstretched arm. Therefore the LORD your God commanded you to keep the Sabbath day.” — [Deuteronomy 5:12–15 (ESV)](https://www.esv.org/Deuteronomy+5%3A12-15/)

### Isaiah 56:2–7

> “Blessed is the man who does this … who keeps the Sabbath, not profaning it. … And the foreigners who join themselves to the LORD … everyone who keeps the Sabbath and does not profane it … these I will bring to my holy mountain, and make them joyful in my house of prayer.” — [Isaiah 56:2–7 (ESV)](https://www.esv.org/Isaiah+56%3A2-7/)

### Isaiah 58:13–14

> “If you turn back your foot from the Sabbath, from doing your pleasure on my holy day … then you shall take delight in the LORD, and I will make you ride on the heights of the earth.” — [Isaiah 58:13–14 (ESV)](https://www.esv.org/Isaiah+58%3A13-14/)

### Jeremiah 17:19–27

> “Thus said the LORD to me: ‘Go and stand in the People’s Gate … and say: Hear the word of the LORD … Take care for the sake of your lives, and do not bear a burden on the Sabbath day … but keep the Sabbath day holy, as I commanded your fathers.’” — [Jeremiah 17:19–27 (ESV)](https://www.esv.org/Jeremiah+17%3A19-27/)

### Ezekiel 20:12–24

> “Moreover, I gave them my Sabbaths, as a sign between me and them, that they might know that I am the LORD who sanctifies them. … Keep my Sabbaths holy that they may be a sign between me and you, that you may know that I am the LORD your God.” — [Ezekiel 20:12–24 (ESV)](https://www.esv.org/Ezekiel+20%3A12-24/)

### Nehemiah 13:15–22

> “In those days I saw in Judah people treading winepresses on the Sabbath … Then I confronted the nobles of Judah and said to them, ‘What is this evil thing that you are doing, profaning the Sabbath day?’ … ‘Why is the house of God forsaken?’ … ‘I commanded the Levites that they should purify themselves and come and guard the gates, to keep the Sabbath day holy.’” — [Nehemiah 13:15–22 (ESV)](https://www.esv.org/Nehemiah+13%3A15-22/)
