# Unleavened Bread

One-event draft. Shared calendar and cross-feast evidence remain in [Feasts](../feasts.draft.md).

## Biblical names and status

“the Feast of Unleavened Bread to the LORD.” — Leviticus 23:6 (ESV)

## Purpose and symbolism

“And you shall observe the Feast of Unleavened Bread, for on this very day I brought your hosts out of the land of Egypt. Therefore you shall observe this day, throughout your generations, as a statute forever.” — Exodus 12:17 (ESV)

## When

Exodus 12:18: first month, fourteenth day at evening through twenty-first day at evening.

“In the first month, on the fourteenth day of the month at twilight, is the LORD’s Passover. And on the fifteenth day of the same month is the Feast of Unleavened Bread to the LORD; for seven days you shall eat unleavened bread.” — [Leviticus 23:5–6 (ESV)](https://www.esv.org/Leviticus+23%3A5-6/)

Verse 6 applies its fifteenth-day date to Unleavened Bread.

## Requirements

### Food

Exodus 12:15 requires seven days of unleavened bread and removal of leaven.

### Sacrifices/offerings

Numbers 28:16–25.

### Assembly/place/private-public

Exodus 12:16 requires holy assemblies on first and seventh days.

### Fasting/self-affliction

No feast-specific command stated.

### Rest/work

Exodus 12:16; Leviticus 23:7–8.

## Forever clause and audience

Exodus 12:17 qualifies seven-day observance.

## Biblical celebration examples

Exodus 12:39; Joshua 5:11; 2 Chronicles 30:13,21; 35:17; Ezra 6:22; Acts 12:3; 1 Corinthians 5:8.
