# Eighth-Day Assembly

The Eighth-Day Assembly immediately follows the seven-day [Feast of Booths](../feasts/booths.md), also called [Sukkot](../feasts/booths.md). Although connected by date, Scripture identifies the eighth day as a solemn assembly and day of rest.

> “For **seven days** you shall present food offerings to the LORD. **On the eighth day** you shall hold a holy convocation and present a food offering to the LORD. It is a solemn assembly; you shall not do any ordinary work.” — Leviticus 23:36 (ESV)

> “On the fifteenth day of the seventh month, when you have gathered in the produce of the land, you shall celebrate the feast of the LORD **seven days**. On the first day shall be a solemn rest, and **on the eighth day** shall be a solemn rest.” — Leviticus 23:39 (ESV)

Leviticus 23:36,39; Numbers 29:35.

## Requirements

### Food

No feast-specific command stated.

### Sacrifices/offerings

Scripture also prescribes the sacrifices for the Eighth-Day Assembly:

> “For seven days you shall present food offerings to the LORD. On the eighth day you shall hold a holy convocation and present **a food offering to the LORD**. It is a solemn assembly; you shall not do any ordinary work.” — Leviticus 23:36 (ESV)

> “But you shall offer **a burnt offering, a food offering**, with a pleasing aroma to the LORD: **one bull, one ram, seven male lambs a year old without blemish**, and **the grain offering and drink offerings** for the bull, for the ram, and for the lambs, in the prescribed quantities; also **one male goat for a sin offering**; besides **the regular burnt offering and its grain offering and its drink offering**.” — Numbers 29:36–38 (ESV)

### Assembly/place/private-public

Holy convocation and solemn assembly.

### Fasting/self-affliction

No feast-specific command stated.

### Rest/work

> “On the eighth day you shall have a solemn assembly. You shall **not do any ordinary work**.” — Numbers 29:35 (ESV)

## Biblical Celebration Examples

Scripture records the Eighth-Day Assembly being observed after both the temple dedication and [the Feast of Booths](../feasts/booths.md):

> “And **on the eighth day** they held a **solemn assembly**, for they had kept the dedication of the altar **seven days** and **the feast seven days**.” — 2 Chronicles 7:9 (ESV)

> “And day by day, from the first day to the last day, he read from the Book of the Law of God. They kept **the feast seven days**, and **on the eighth day** there was a **solemn assembly**, according to the rule.” — Nehemiah 8:18 (ESV)

John may describe another celebration connected with this assembly, although the passage does not explicitly identify the day as the eighth:

> “**On the last day of the feast, the great day**, Jesus stood up and cried out, ‘If anyone thirsts, let him come to me and drink. Whoever believes in me, as the Scripture has said, “Out of his heart will flow rivers of living water.”’ Now this he said about the Spirit, whom those who believed in him were to receive, for as yet the Spirit had not been given, because Jesus was not yet glorified.” — John 7:37–39 (ESV)
