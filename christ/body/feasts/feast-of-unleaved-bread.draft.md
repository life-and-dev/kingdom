# Feast of Unleaved Bread

> You shall keep the Feast of Unleavened Bread. As I commanded you, you shall eat unleavened bread for seven days at the appointed time in the month of rAbib, for in it you came out of Egypt. -- Exodus 23:15 (ESV)

## Conclusion

The verse records Israel’s commanded Feast of Unleavened Bread; see [Feast of Unleaved Bread](#feast-of-unleaved-bread). Christians may choose a household or community meal with unleavened bread and read 1 Corinthians 5:8, “let us therefore celebrate the festival” (ESV), in its stated moral context. No other food is prescribed for a modern Christian observance. These are ideas only, not biblical commands or requirements for Christians.
