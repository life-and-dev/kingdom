# Roles in the Body of Christ

This article examines biblical responsibilities within the Body of Christ across the assembly, workplace, and household. Its central theme is that Christ is head, while each person bears distinct responsibilities marked by love, service, and accountability to Him. The sections below gather passages on Christ’s authority and the conduct of overseers, teachers, members, workers, and households.

## Christ

> He *[the God of Jesus Christ]* worked in [Christ](../../christ.md) when He raised him from the dead and seated him at His right hand in the heavenly places, far above all rule and authority and power and dominion, and above every [name that is named](https://word.ofgod.info/terms/name), not only in this age but also in the one to come. And He put all things under his feet and gave him as **head over all things to the church, which is [his body](../body.md)**, the fullness of Him who fills all in all. — Ephesians 1:20-23 (ESV)

## Assembly

Each role in an assembly is defined by conduct rather than status. Embodying sound doctrine through [love](https://word.ofgod.info/terms/love) and building others up through example, teaching, service, or mutual care. As each member works according to their gift and maturity, the whole body grows together in Christ.

### Members

Jesus gave the following command:

> A new commandment I give to you, that you **[love](https://word.ofgod.info/terms/love) one another: just as I have loved you, you also are to love one another**. By this all people will know that you are my disciples, if you have **love for one another**.” — John 13:34-35

The Greek word translated as "love" when Jesus or the apostles refer to disciples or members that should "love one another" is [agapé](https://biblehub.com/greek/26.htm). This is not emotional or romantic love, but instead agapé means selfless, sacrificial, unconditional love. In other words, to make a deliberate choice to seek the other's well being despite their attitude or performance.

For example Peter associates "loving one another earnestly" with "fiery trial" and "sufferings":

> Above all, keep **loving one another earnestly**, since **love** covers a multitude of sins. Show hospitality to one another without grumbling. As each has received a gift, use it to serve one another, as good stewards of God’s varied grace: whoever speaks, as one who speaks oracles of God; whoever serves, as one who serves by the strength that God supplies⁠—in order that in everything God may be glorified through Jesus Christ. To him belong glory and dominion forever and ever. Amen.
>
> Beloved, do not be surprised at the **fiery trial** when it comes upon you to test you, as though something strange were happening to you. But rejoice insofar as you share Christ’s **sufferings**, that you may also rejoice and be glad when his glory is revealed.
>
> — 1 Peter 4:8-13

> For by the grace given to me I say to everyone among you **not to think of himself more highly than he ought to think**, but to think with sober judgment, each according to the measure of faith that God has assigned. Having gifts that differ according to the grace given to us, let us use them: if prophecy, in proportion to our faith; if service, in our serving; the one who teaches, in his teaching; the one who exhorts, in his exhortation; the one who contributes, in generosity; the one who leads, with zeal; the one who does acts of mercy, with cheerfulness.

> Let **love be genuine**. Abhor what is evil; hold fast to what is good. **Love one another with brotherly affection**.
>

> **Rejoice** in hope, **be patient** in tribulation, **be constant in prayer**.

## Households

Within a household, [husbands](#husbands) lead through sacrificial love, [wives](#wives) help through faithful support and wise stewardship, [parents](#parents) provide godly instruction without provoking their children, and [children](#children) obey, honour, and learn from their parents. Though their duties differ, each serves under Christ so the household grows in faithfulness, wisdom, and love.

### Husbands

In [marriage](marriage.md#accountability-before-god), a husband bears accountable leadership under Christ. This headship does not make him superior or permit tyranny. It calls him to love his wife as his own body, nourishing and cherishing her through [sacrificial, servant leadership](marriage.md#servant-leader).

He lives with her in understanding, honours her needs as an [equal co-heir](marriage.md#conduct-honour-and-co-heirship), and avoids harshness. He keeps [covenant faithfulness](marriage.md#covenant-faithfulness), shares mutual [conjugal duty](marriage.md#sexual-passions), and accepts responsibility for instructing children without provoking them. His headship gives no authority to command sin. He does not compromise on her [obedience to God](marriage.md#obeying-god).

### Wives

In [marriage](marriage.md#help-in-companionship), a wife is a helper. She is an ally who brings strength and support, not a slave or inferior, but as a critical role in the household. She submits to and respects her own husband, showing [gentle, respectful, and pure conduct](marriage.md#conduct-honour-and-co-heirship).

She faithfully cares for her household and exercises [wise stewardship](marriage.md#an-excellent-wife). She keeps [covenant faithfulness](marriage.md#covenant-faithfulness) and shares mutual [conjugal duty](marriage.md#sexual-passions) as an equal co-heir. She does not compromise on her [obedience to God](marriage.md#obeying-god).

## Workplace

Although masters and servants have different duties under Christ, both serve same Lord and must answer to Him. He does not favor one person over another.

### Masters

> **Rendering service with a good will as to the Lord and not to man**, knowing that whatever good anyone does, this he will receive back from the Lord, whether he is a bondservant or is free. Masters, do the same to them, and **stop your threatening**, knowing that he who is both their Master and yours is in heaven, and that there is no partiality with Him. — Ephesians 6:7-9 (ESV)

> Masters, **treat your bondservants justly and fairly**, knowing that you also have a Master in heaven. — Colossians 4:1 (ESV)

### Servants

> Bondservants, **obey your earthly masters** with fear and trembling, with a sincere heart, as you would Christ, not by the way of eye-service, as people-pleasers, but as bondservants of Christ, doing **the will of God from the heart**, **rendering service with a good will as to the Lord and not to man**, knowing that whatever good anyone does, this he will receive back from the Lord, whether he is a bondservant or is free. — Ephesians 6:5-8 (ESV)

> Bondservants, **obey in everything those who are your earthly masters**, not by way of eye-service, as people-pleasers, but with sincerity of heart, fearing the Lord. **Whatever you do, work heartily, as for the Lord** and not for men, knowing that **from the Lord you will receive the inheritance as your reward**. You are serving the Lord Christ. For the wrongdoer will be paid back for the wrong he has done, and there is no partiality. — Colossians 3:22-25 (ESV)

> Bondservants are to **be submissive to their own masters in everything**; they are to be well-pleasing, not argumentative, not pilfering, but showing all good faith, so that in everything they may adorn the doctrine of God our Savior. — Titus 2:9-10 (ESV)

> Servants, be **subject to your masters with all respect**, not only to the good and gentle but also to the unjust. For this is a gracious thing, when, mindful of God, one endures sorrows while suffering unjustly. For what credit is it if, when you sin and are beaten for it, you endure? But if when you do good and suffer for it you endure, this is a gracious thing in the sight of God. For to this you have been called, because Christ also suffered for you, leaving you an example, so that you might follow in his steps. He committed no sin, neither was deceit found in his mouth. When he was reviled, he did not revile in return; when he suffered, he did not threaten, but continued entrusting himself to Him who judges justly. — 1 Peter 2:18-23 (ESV)

## Conclusion

[Christ](#christ) is head of His body. Within the [assembly](#assembly), [households](#households) and [workplace](#workplace), distinct responsibilities are carried out in [love](https://word.ofgod.info/terms/love) and service, with every person accountable to the same Lord. Through this faithful order, each member helps build up the body in Christ.
